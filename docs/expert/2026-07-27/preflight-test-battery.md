# preflight-test-battery (agent report, 2026-07-26 pre-flight sweep)

## Summary

The measured C=6 budget chain (4.52e12 emissions, ~7.6 days, ~45 GB) contains no arithmetic errors and the 3->4 and 5->6 concerns raised are quantitatively bounded, but the audit found five unpriced gaps: (1) the cap=1.0e9 layer-4 table has only ~10% headroom over a Chao1 estimate that is formally a lower bound under the heterogeneity its own f-histogram displays, and overflow is std::abort() with no checkpointing anywhere in the engine; (2) the entire 145.7 ns/em constant was measured with a plain u64 accumulator, while C=6 intermediate T values overflow u64 -- the required 2-residue modular payload is unimplemented and unbenchmarked, and raises real RAM to ~77-95 GB, not 45; (3) the 4->5 leg (2.38e12, 53% of budget) is the only load-bearing [est], resting on history-weighted sampling in the wrong measure; (4) depth-5 canonize and big-table insert costs are unmeasured; (5) between minute ~20 (M_3 check) and day ~7.6 (F6(G1)/G2) the run has zero independent integrity anchors. Ten pre-flight tests, each under 1 hour on the 32-core box, close every gap. One genuinely new idea is offered (a per-layer ordered-history mass checksum verified against independent Ryser permanents, giving mid-run integrity at <1% overhead, falsifiable at C=5 in seconds); otherwise the frontier is clean.

=== PART 1: ERRORS AND UNSTATED ASSUMPTIONS IN THE MEASURED BUDGET CHAIN ===

Positive audit results first (independent recomputation, all pass): Chao1 arithmetic (12,995,262 + 2.48e6^2/(2*3.16e6) = 13.97e6; x64 = 8.94e8) correct; kept fraction 39,402,926/2,522,319,152 = 1/64.01 = 2^-6 exactly (hash uniformity confirmed); implied layer-4 in-degree 2.136e12/9.1e8 = 2,347 vs profile prediction 2,589/1.096 = 2,362 (+0.6%, consistent); capture rate 2,347/844.2 = 2.78 matches lambda 2.86; wall arithmetic 4.52e12 x 145.7 ns = 7.62 days correct; the doc's "~45 GB at cap 1.0e9" is exactly right for the CURRENT engine (State = 12 x u16 = 24 B + 8 B u64 T + 4 B stab = 36 B/state x 1e9 + 2^31 x 4 B index = 44.6 GB) -- but see finding F2. Chain closures (+0.06% C=5, +0.16% C=6) verified.

F1. [3->4 fan sample bias -- the question asked] LOW RISK, quantitatively bounded. The 2.136e12 total is a mean over a 20,000-state systematic (strided) sample from the exact insertion-ordered M_3 table. Three independent bounds: (a) hard cap -- the L=3 free graph is 6-regular on 12 vertices, so per-state fan <= Bregman-Minc (6!)^2 = 518,400 = 2.99x the sample mean 173,291; even if every unsampled state sat at the cap the total could not exceed 6.39e12, and the observed max is only 237,312 (1.37x mean); (b) two independent strided samples exist and agree to 0.3% -- the m4probe's 14,600 parents emitted 2,522,319,152 tables = 172,762/parent vs the 20k sample's 173,291; (c) stride truncation (stride = floor(12,324,872/20,000) = 616 excludes the last ~4,872 late-discovered states) contributes at most 4,872 x 518,400 = 2.5e9 = 0.1% of total even adversarially. Residual risk is only stride-vs-hash periodicity, worth one 5-minute randomized re-sample (T8).

F2. [Arithmetic width -- unstated gap between measured engine and production engine] CONFIRMED GAP. layer_dp_gate.cpp accumulates T in a plain u64 (std::vector<u64> T, line ~386); primes P1 = 2^61-1, P2 < 2^62 (lines 642-643) are used only by the rank code, and the comment at line 1086 admits "C=6 totals would need wider". Final-layer C=6 T_q = m_q F_q reaches ~2^100 (m up to |G| = 12! x 6! x 2^6 = 2.2e13, F up to ~5e16), and intermediate T values are larger, so the production DP must run modularly -- yet every ns/emission number in the doc was measured with the u64 build. Sharpening the doc's "2-3 primes": exactly 2 residues suffice, because only final-layer T_q ever needs reconstruction (T_q < 2^100 < P1*P2 ~ 2^123; per-class F_q < 2^63; N is then computed exactly in multiprecision from 63,199 reconstructed F values). A 3rd residue is purely the self-check. Memory consequence with 2 residues at a safe cap of 1.35e9 (see F3): 1.35e9 x 44 B + 2^32 x 4 B index (17.2 GB) = ~77 GB, plus layer-5 table (~10 GB at cap 2.5e8) and layer-3 (<1 GB) = ~90 GB peak. Fits the 125.7 GB box, but the doc's "40-50 GB" understates the production configuration by ~2x. Test T4.

F3. [M_4 capacity cliff] HIGHEST OPERATIONAL RISK. Capacity overflow is std::abort() (lines 464-468), the engine has no checkpointing whatsoever (grep confirms: only a final --dump CSV), and cap = 1.0e9 leaves ~10% headroom over M_4 ~ 9.1e8. The estimate is bias-suspect in exactly the dangerous direction: Chao1 is formally a lower bound under in-degree heterogeneity, and the probe's own hit histogram proves heterogeneity -- successive f-ratio lambda estimates (k+1)f_{k+1}/f_k = 2.55, 2.76, 2.90 (would be constant ~2.86 if homogeneous). A negative-binomial fit to those ratios (r ~ 13.5, q ~ 0.176, P(0) = 0.073 vs Poisson 0.057) gives M_4 ~ 8.97e8 -- reassuringly close to Chao1, suggesting mild heterogeneity -- but the C=5 calibration (-1.7%) was lambda-matched, not heterogeneity-matched, and NB is one mixture family. If true M_4 is 4-10% above 9.1e8, the abort fires near the END of 3->4, ~3.5 days in, losing everything. Fix costs nothing: cap = 1.35e9 (index becomes 2^32 = 17.2 GB; u32 slot indices still fit). Tests T1, T5, T6. Note the good news found in code audit: init_fixed sizes the index at >= 2x cap rounded up to a power of two, so index load factor stays <= 0.47 even at full capacity -- probe chains stay short; the cliff is the abort, not probing.

F4. [4->5 estimate calibration -- the question asked] THE WEAKEST MEASURED LINK: 2.38e12 = Chao1 M_4 (9.1e8) x history-weighted mean tables (2,876/1.096 = 2,624) is 53% of the total budget and is the only load-bearing [est]. The measure is wrong (2,000 history-weighted permanent samples, not uniform over canonical states), and the same-day layer-3 analog missed M_3 by 1.33x. Mitigating evidence: the FAN component (as opposed to in-degree/state-count components) was accurate where testable -- L3 history-weighted prediction 197,356/1.152 = 171,316 vs uniform-measured 173,291 (+1.2%), and C=5 L4 predicted 26.58 vs actual 27.01 (-1.6%) -- so +-10% is the realistic band, not 1.33x. But it is measurable for free: the m4probe's windowed distinct children are a hash-uniform sample of layer-4 canonical states, i.e. EXACTLY the uniform measure needed, unbiased by in-degree. Test T2 converts this leg to [measured +-1%] in minutes. Related internal tension: M_5 ~ 7e7 requires the ~1.4x in-degree calibration factor (uncalibrated: 2.38e12/(27,263/1.119) = 9.8e7); set layer-5 cap >= 2.5e8 (10 GB) so either value is safe.

F5. [5->6 "exact 64" -- the question asked] The 64 is labelled MATCHINGS (C disjoint K_{2,2}, 2^C matchings, exact); emissions everywhere else in the budget are K_c-MERGED TABLES. Measured C=5 ground truth: 462,403/17,120 = 27.01 tables/state vs 2^5 = 32 matchings (merge 1.185, matching the profile's 1.204). So C=6 will see ~64/1.119 = 57 tables/state, not 64. Budget impact: nil (5->6 is ~0.1% of total, and 64 OVERestimates; ~5e9 is if anything high). Real risk is only an in-run assert or normalization wired to "emissions == 64 x M_5", which would false-fail; the C=5 gate exactness proves the normalization itself is table-based and correct. Action: grep the production build for hardcoded 2^C fan assumptions; use 2^C only as an upper bound.

F6. [Per-emission constant extrapolation] The 145.7 ns/em was measured on 2->3 (child table 12.3M states, 0.6 GB, 2^25 index -- cache-resident). Two unmeasured deltas: (a) 3->4 makes 2.136e12 accesses against a 45-77 GB table; 99.96% of emissions are accumulates into EXISTING keys (1 - 9.1e8/2.136e12), i.e. ~2 random DRAM lines each (index probe + T atomic add) -- good MLP behavior expected, but +50-150 ns/em is plausible, stretching 3->4 from 3.5 to 4.5-6 days; (b) depth-5 canonize (4->5 children) has never been timed at C=6 -- the m4probe timed depth-4 canonize at 114 ns/em wall (2.522e9 canonizations in 288 s on 24 threads), which is actually FASTER than 2->3's 145.7, encouraging but not evidence for depth 5. Tests T2, T3. Honest planning band after these effects plus F2's payload: 8-14 days, with 7.6 as the floor.

F7. [Mid-run integrity blindness] All independent in-run anchors (772 states, mass 20,338,525, emissions 2,605,194,602, M_3 = 12,324,872) are consumed in the first ~20 minutes. F6(G1)/F6(G2) are only computable AFTER 5->6 completes at day ~7.6 -- the doc's "long before any total is trusted" is misleading on timing. Layer-3 mass 566,455,903,200 is self-generated (no independent value exists); layer-4/5 masses are unknown. Between minute 20 and day 7.6 nothing independent checks the run, on a week-long, ~90 GB, (likely non-ECC) computation where a flipped KEY bit is silent even under multi-prime CRT (both residues share the corrupted key). See new idea N1 and tests T7, T9, T10. Also unstated: the G1/G2 bridge -- matching those two factorization_orbit classes to layer-DP complete-class keys -- is proven as a mechanism at C=5 (355 triples) but has not been built for C=6; build it BEFORE the run or the end-of-run check can be vacuous (T7).

F8. [Benign but worth recording] The m3 log shows "holes=1" at 2->3 while the doc says "zero capacity holes" -- audited: holes are abandoned CAS-race slots (stab == 0, skipped by consumers, real_size excludes them); results are unaffected, but holes CONSUME capacity, another small reason for headroom at 3->4's 24-thread contention (expect O(10^2-10^3) holes).

=== PART 3: NEW IDEAS (limit two) ===

N1 (one genuinely new idea, with < 1 h falsification): PER-LAYER ORDERED-HISTORY CHECKSUM AGAINST INDEPENDENT PERMANENTS. Carry a second per-state accumulator h_x (mod p, +8 B/state, ~10 GB at layer 4) propagating labelled ordered-history mass (the labelled-matching multiplicity transport, i.e. the chain-closure quantity of Section 4.1 made exact per state instead of in expectation). Then at every layer boundary the identity sum_{y in L+1} h_y = sum_{x in L} h_x * per(FREE(rep(x))) is checkable with the right-hand permanents computed by Ryser -- a code path completely independent of the emission enumerator and canonizer. Cost at the C=6 layer-3 boundary: 12.3M Ryser permanents of 12x12 6-regular matrices (~5e4 flops each, ~6e11 flops, 1-2 min on 24 cores); at the layer-4 boundary ~9.1e8 permanents of 4-regular matrices (~1-2 h on 24 cores, or a 1% strided-sample version in minutes with an exact-sum variant reserved for the layer-3 and layer-5 boundaries). This closes F7's 7-day blindness window: any lost, duplicated, or corrupted accumulation surfaces at the next layer boundary instead of at day 7.6. FALSIFICATION TEST (< 1 h, mostly seconds): implement h in the C=4/C=5 gate (4-30 s runs) and check the identity exactly at every layer, including that sum h at the final layer equals the independently known A_C chain product (10! x 440,192 x ... at C=5). If no normalization of h makes the identity exact through C=5, the idea is dead at zero cost to the schedule.

N2: None offered. Every other candidate examined either reduces to a closed route (uniform layer-4 sampling is a calibration test, not an idea; staged 3->4-first commitment is run structuring, folded into T5's checkpoint design; the defect-2 checksum is already the doc's own open probe, executed as T10) or lacks a sub-hour falsification. Apart from N1 and the ten tests above, the frontier is clean: no closed route deserves reopening, and no error was found in any number tagged [exact] or [measured].

## Verdict

GO WITH CONDITIONS: the measured budget chain is arithmetically sound and internally consistent (no error found in any [exact] or [measured] figure; the three flagged concerns -- 3->4 strided-fan bias, 4->5 calibration, 5->6 tables-vs-matchings -- are respectively bounded (<=1% realistic, Bregman-Minc-capped at 3x), the genuine weak link (53% of budget, fixable to +-1% by test T2), and a documentation/assert hazard with zero budget impact). But the run as currently configured is NOT commit-ready: the modular payload is unimplemented (all constants were measured on a u64 build that overflows at C=6), the layer-4 cap has ~10% headroom over a lower-bound-biased estimate with abort-on-overflow and no checkpointing, real RAM is ~77-95 GB not 45, realistic wall is 8-14 days not 7.6, and the run is integrity-blind from minute 20 to day 7.6. All ten pre-flight tests fit inside one working day on the 32-core box; commit only after T1-T7 pass (T8-T10 and N1 strongly recommended).
